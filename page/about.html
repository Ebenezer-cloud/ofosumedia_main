Who We Are

Ofosu Media Limited is operating under Freedom Radio 89.3 in the Eastern capital, Koforidua, in the Ataa Plaza building near Total 6 Koforidua. The radio was founded by Nana Yaw Ofosu, who was the CEO and a shareholder in the company.
